This is a bank program that has some of the basic action you can do with an account

1. You're going to be able to open an account
2. Get the information of said account
3. Change the Pin of the account and all accounts made
4. Deposit money into an account
5. Transfer money between accounts
6. Withdraw money from account
7. Withdraw money in 5 dollar increments aka 5s,10s and 20s
8. Deposit change pennies, nickels, dimes, quarters, half and whole dollars
9. Close the account(s)
10. Add monthly interest to all accounts
11. Close out the session
